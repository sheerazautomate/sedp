## Reporting hierarchy — state it before the first number

```
District Layyah  (1,223 schools)
├── M-EE  Male Elementary    556 schools → 3 tehsils → 57 markaz → schools
├── W-EE  Female Elementary  467 schools → 3 tehsils → 43 markaz → schools
└── SE    Secondary          200 high schools → one district block
```

The wing sits above the tehsil. Only the two elementary wings have tehsil and markaz layers; the
secondary wing is one block of high schools and is **never** split tehsil-wise. Every table in this
deck follows that order, and the levels reconcile: elementary 1,023 schools / 159,979 students /
3,627 gap, plus secondary 200 / 106,838 / 7,011, equals the district's 1,223 / 266,817 / 10,638.

## 1 · Cover (1 min)
**266,817 students enrolled, 33.9% of the way to a 277,455 target.** Set expectations: this is not
a district-wide failure story — it is a story about where the 10,638 missing students sit.

## 2 · Executive summary (3 min)
1. **33.9%** of the target increment — 5,451 added, 10,638 still needed.
2. **19.9 pp** gender gap in achievement (boys 43.9%, girls 24.0%); girls are 59% of the need.
3. **66%** of the gap sits in the secondary block — 200 high schools teaching 40% of students.
4. **30%** of the gap sits in 20 high schools; closing them lifts the district to 53.6%.

## 3 · Scope & method (3 min)
Read the hierarchy strip left to right and say plainly: wing first, then tehsil, then markaz, then
school. Current enrollment from SIS; baseline and target from Grades. Achievement =
(current − baseline) ÷ (target − baseline), uncapped, computed on summed school values.
Flag early: **799 schools have a target only 1–10 students above baseline**, so school-level
percentages swing from −800% to +3,000%. Use them as flags, not league tables.

### Answers to likely questions

**“Why is the district at 33.9% when the median school is 66.7%?”**
Achievement is enrollment-weighted. Half the schools are small and doing well; the thousands of
students in large high schools are not.

**“Why don't you show secondary results by tehsil?”**
Because secondary is not a tehsil structure. Its 200 high schools are administered as one block, so
splitting them by tehsil would invent a layer that does not exist. Location is kept in the CSV for
follow-up only.

**“Does the arithmetic reconcile?”**
Yes. Three elementary tehsils (495 + 368 + 160 schools) = 1,023 elementary schools; elementary
159,979 students + secondary 106,838 = 266,817; gaps 3,627 + 7,011 = 10,638.

**“Why do some schools show −300% or +3,000%?”**
Their target is only a few students above baseline, so each student is worth tens of points. Rank by
students needed, not percentage.

**“Why 100 markaz and not 101?”**
The source lists three “Secondary Wing” rows as markaz; these are wing aggregates, not AEO clusters.
They are excluded, and the secondary block replaces them.

**“Is any of this a data problem?”**
Partly: 99 schools record no girls, 66 no boys, 2 no enrollment. Workstream 5 settles that before
the next target cycle.
